# Review Framework

## Time Basis

Never review without stating:

- Posting time.
- Analysis cutoff time.
- Elapsed time.
- Data window or source.

If the cutoff time is not explicit:

- Use screenshot filename time when the filename contains a timestamp.
- Otherwise use file modified time.
- Otherwise ask Liu Tao only if the conclusion would materially change.

## Core Diagnosis Order

Use this order for natural short-video review:

1. Exposure / playback volume.
2. First 2 seconds or early jump rate.
3. 5-second completion.
4. Completion rate and average watch time.
5. Engagement: likes, comments, shares, saves.
6. Profile visits and follows.
7. Product clicks, add-to-cart, orders, ROI when cart or paid traffic exists.

Do not skip early retention. If 2-second jump or average watch time is terrible, later metrics are usually downstream symptoms.

## Correction Rules

### Very High 2-Second Jump / Average Watch Around 1 Second

Likely problem:

- First frame is not giving a reason to stay.
- Opening visual does not match title/hook promise.
- Hook is only in the title, not visible or audible in the first seconds.
- Video starts too slowly or too static.

Next correction:

- Change first frame or start from a stronger second.
- Add one short screen text for 0-2.2 seconds.
- Rewrite the golden-3-second voiceover as a real trial-wear judgment.
- Make the first visible frame prove the title claim.
- Do not test paid traffic yet.

Women's clothing examples:

- `微胖穿波点裤，先看裤腿`
- `别只看正面，先看腰线`
- `这套最怕显胯，先看侧面`

### 5-Second Completion Is 0 Or Extremely Low

Likely problem:

- Hook is weak.
- First 3 seconds have no visual progression.
- The video is too much like a static product display.

Next correction:

- Shorten the video or cut directly into the full-body proof.
- Use one clear visual question: waistline, skirt shape, trouser drape, commute scene, or body-shape fit.
- Avoid full-screen captions and heavy sales copy.

### Playback Low But Retention Unknown

Likely problem:

- Cold-start account.
- Title/hashtags not matching target audience.
- Cover/first frame does not trigger information-flow interest.

Next correction:

- Do not declare product failure yet.
- Compare same product with a different title/hook/template.
- Record title/hashtag variant and observe next video.

### Playback Exists But Engagement Weak

Likely problem:

- Viewer can watch but has no reason to react.
- Selling point is too broad.
- The video does not create a judgment question.

Next correction:

- Add a sharper hook or comment-triggering angle.
- Use real trial-wear language, not generic praise.
- For women's clothing, anchor the problem to body shape, commute, waistline, drape, fabric, or fit risk.

### Saves/Comments Exist But Product Click Weak

Likely problem:

- Content is treated as outfit inspiration, not a buyable item.
- Title/hashtags are too aesthetic and not product-specific.
- Cart cue is weak.

Next correction:

- Next video can keep the natural style but add product-facing title words.
- Add a light cart cue only after the content reason is clear.
- Do not turn the whole video into hard selling.

### Product Click Exists But Orders Weak

Likely problem:

- Product detail page, price, reviews, size chart, or trust is weak.
- The video created interest but did not answer purchase doubts.

Next correction:

- Review product page, reviews, size/fit, and refund risk.
- Make the next video answer a purchase doubt: size, fabric, transparency, waist fit, trouser drape, or real scene.

### Paid Traffic Exists

Separate:

- Natural content signal: retention, engagement, comments, profile visits.
- Paid signal: spend, CTR, CPC, conversion cost, ROI, order count, refund risk.

If natural early retention is weak, do not solve it only by increasing spend.

## Decision Labels

Use one of these final labels:

- `继续观察`: data is too early or incomplete.
- `同商品加做`: clear signal exists and correction is specific.
- `换开头重测`: early retention problem is dominant.
- `换标题话题重测`: playback entry is weak but video may be usable.
- `换模板重测`: visual structure is the core issue.
- `暂不挂车/暂不投流`: content signal is not ready.
- `暂停商品`: multiple videos fail and product/page risks are high.
